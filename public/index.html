THE COMPLETE STORY

History of IPS West Bengal

1921

Kolkata. Where it began.

West Bengal has played a major role in the development of pharmacology in India. The roots of pharmacological research in the country are strongly connected with Kolkata, especially the School of Tropical Medicine, where Col. Ram Nath Chopra worked as the first Professor of Pharmacology in 1921. He is widely regarded as the “Father of Indian Pharmacology” because of his pioneering research on indigenous medicines and experimental pharmacology.

1966

A society takes shape.

The Indian Pharmacological Society (IPS) is one of the leading professional organizations for pharmacologists in India. The society was formed to promote research, education, and scientific development in pharmacology and allied biomedical sciences.

According to the official history of IPS, the movement for establishing an independent pharmacological society began in 1966 during the Annual Conference of the Association of Physiologists and Pharmacologists of India at Patna. The first official conference of IPS was held in January 1969.

1969

A national conversation.

The first official conference of the Indian Pharmacological Society was held in January 1969.

The West Bengal branch of the Indian Pharmacological Society later emerged as an important regional chapter that encouraged academic activities, conferences, and collaboration among pharmacologists, medical teachers, researchers, and students in eastern India. The branch has organized scientific meetings, workshops, and annual conferences to advance pharmacological education and research in the state.

2026

The next chapter continues.

One notable recent event was the 2026 Eastern Zonal IPSWBCON, held in March 2026, featuring academic sessions, paper presentations, and research collaborations.

INDIAN PHARMACOLOGICAL SOCIETY WEST BENGAL STATE BRANCH

A distinguished legacy of academic exchange.

The West Bengal State Branch of the Indian Pharmacological Society (IPSWB) promotes excellence in pharmacological education, research, and clinical practice. Over the years, the society has successfully organized 33 State Conferences and hosted 5 National Conferences in West Bengal. These conferences have served as important academic platforms for pharmacologists, researchers, clinicians, healthcare professionals, and students across India.

Major past events

Event type Conference details Highlights
State conferences Successfully organized 33 Annual State Conferences across various institutions in West Bengal. Scientific sessions, poster presentations, young researcher awards, CME activities, and collaborative discussions.
National conferences Successfully hosted 5 National Conferences of IPS. Participation from eminent pharmacologists, national delegates, keynote lectures, translational research discussions, and industry–academia interaction.
Academic workshops Workshops and symposiums in pharmacovigilance, clinical pharmacology, research methodology, and drug safety. Skill development and capacity building for students and professionals.
Student activities PG paper presentations, quiz competitions, and research forums. Encouraged young researchers and future pharmacologists.
